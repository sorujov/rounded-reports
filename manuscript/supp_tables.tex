\section{Additional simulation results}\label{supp:tables}

The designs and mechanisms are those of Section~\ref{sec:sim}. Rejection frequencies are at the five per cent level. Tables~S1 and~S2 use 200 replications per cell, for which a frequency of $0.05$ has a Monte Carlo standard error of about $0.015$; Tables~S3--S5 use 400 replications for unimodal and 200 for bimodal designs, as in Table~\ref{tab:sim} (standard errors of about $0.011$ and $0.015$ at $0.05$); Table~S6 uses 60.
