\section{Discussion}\label{sec:disc}

\paragraph{Rounding is not the only source of atoms.}
The model treats every pile-up as something the respondent did. A minimum wage times full-time hours, a flat-rate pension, a benefit scale and a top code put genuine mass at particular amounts, and a density with such an atom is not unimodal in any useful sense. The two differ in their arithmetic: rounding places mass at every multiple of a level, an institutional amount at one value or a few unrelated ones. The mixture of Proposition~\ref{prop:totient} describes the first and the test of Theorem~\ref{thm:spec} reports when the reports are not explained by it; a procedure estimating both kinds of atom jointly remains to be developed.

\paragraph{The variable matters more than the survey.}
A sum of amounts reported by different household members, net of simulated taxes and divided by an equivalence scale, retains little of the arithmetic of its components, and what it retains can mislead (Section~\ref{sec:data}); agencies that publish constructed totals could release the reported components alongside them.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The identification argument assumes that a level is chosen and the amount then rounded to it, which excludes respondents who report a round number unrelated to their income; that the levels lie in a known candidate ladder, protected by including every plausible round amount and by the specification test, which detects an omitted level dividing $W$; and that residues are close to uniform given the level, which fails when income is narrow relative to $W$, a failure the specification test detects and the share of reports below $W$ foreshadows. The validity of the cell test is pointwise and for a fixed number of cells; uniform validity over the boundary of the cone, and theory for a number of cells growing with the sample, are open. The inverted test has no asymptotic theory beyond Theorem~\ref{thm:invert} and should be used only when the specification test passes. Rounding propensity varies with education, pay frequency and proxy response; a statement about such a subpopulation rests on the mechanism within it, and since Proposition~\ref{prop:totient} conditions only on the level, the estimator extends to covariates directly.
